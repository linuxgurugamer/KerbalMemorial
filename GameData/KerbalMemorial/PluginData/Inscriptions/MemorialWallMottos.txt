### **Kerbal Memorial Wall Mottos**

Their missions ended. Our mission continues.
They gave their altitude for our tomorrow.
In memory of those who launched but never landed.
Many missions. Many heroes. One Kerbin.
For every Kerbal who dared to reach the stars.
We remember every name, every mission, every sacrifice.
The cost of discovery shall never be forgotten.
To those who gave their lives in the pursuit of science, exploration, and questionable engineering.
They may have left our crew, but they will never leave our hearts.
We launch in their memory. We explore in their honor.
Their rockets may have fallen, but their dreams still soar.
Beyond Kerbin, beyond the Mun, beyond our reach, but never forgotten.
Every successful mission stands upon the courage of those who came before.
In honor of those whose final destination was among the stars.
They ventured into the unknown so that others might return.
Their journeys were measured in kilometers. Their courage was immeasurable.
For those who gave everything to advance Kerbalkind.
Not all heroes returned to Kerbin.
Through triumph and tragedy, the spirit of exploration endures.
Their final countdown has passed, but their legacy will never expire.
