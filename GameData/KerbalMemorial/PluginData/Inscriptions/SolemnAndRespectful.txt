### **Solemn and respectful**

Forever among the stars.
Gone from Kerbin, but never forgotten.
Their final mission is complete.
In memory of those who dared to explore.
Their courage carried us beyond the skies.
They gave everything for the journey.
May their spirits orbit forever.
Beyond our reach, but never beyond our hearts.
Their footprints remain among the stars.
We remember those who never returned.
Their mission ended, but their legacy lives on.
For those who reached beyond the horizon.
They flew so others could follow.
No longer with us, forever part of our journey.
The stars shine brighter in their memory.
