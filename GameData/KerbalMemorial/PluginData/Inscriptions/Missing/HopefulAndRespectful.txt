### **Hopeful and respectful**

Missing among the stars, but never forgotten.
Until you return, we wait.
Your journey is not yet over.
Somewhere beyond the horizon.
Lost contact, never lost hope.
Kerbin awaits your return.
Your place among us remains.
May the stars guide you home.
Wherever you are, you are remembered.
Until we meet again on Kerbin.
No distance can diminish our hope.
Your mission remains unfinished.
We await your signal.
Beyond our reach, but never beyond our thoughts.
The light of hope remains lit.
Your crew awaits your return.
Somewhere in the vastness of space.
May your next transmission bring you home.
Unaccounted for, but never forgotten.
The search continues, and so does our hope.
